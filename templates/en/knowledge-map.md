# Knowledge map

One entry per concept_id: the term in the course's language, a plain explanation, the conditions it needs, prerequisites, related_concepts, and sources.

Each source has a source_id, a locator (page, section, or question number), a version, and whether it was verified. Each exam_evidence item has a kind (stated marks, past-paper frequency, teacher hint, or guessed priority) and its source. With no evidence, leave it unknown; do not invent weights.

The learner's questions and mastery belong in the study records. The map describes how the knowledge fits together and does not turn how often something was asked into an exam weight.
